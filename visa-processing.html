Our Visa Processing Service

Sara Overseas Limited provides professional visa processing services ensuring transparency, accuracy, and fast completion. We guide candidates through every step to make the process smooth and stress-free.

01
Document Collection

We collect all required documents such as passport, photos, certificates, and job offer letters.

02
Medical Test

Candidates complete medical tests from authorized medical centers as per embassy requirements.

03
Visa Application

We submit visa applications to the respective embassy with proper documentation and accuracy.

04
Embassy Approval

Embassy reviews the application and grants visa approval after verification.

05
Visa Stamping

Approved visas are stamped on passports according to embassy regulations.

06
Departure & Ticketing

Final travel arrangements including air ticketing and departure briefing.
