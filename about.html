About Sara Overseas Ltd

Who we are & what we stand for

About Us

Sara Overseas Limited is a government approved manpower recruiting agency in Bangladesh, supplying skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workers to various countries across the globe.

We have earned trust through transparency, efficiency, and commitment to international recruitment standards.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide world-class manpower recruitment services by connecting the right talent with the right opportunity.

We aim to ensure ethical recruitment practices, worker welfare, and complete client satisfaction.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a globally recognized manpower recruitment company known for reliability, quality, and professionalism.

We strive to build long-term partnerships with clients and contribute positively to workforce development worldwide.
